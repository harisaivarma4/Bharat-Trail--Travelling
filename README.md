🇮🇳 Bharat Trails – Explore Incredible India
🌍 About the Project

Bharat Trails is a tourism and travel website designed to showcase the beauty, culture, history, and heritage of India. It helps users explore famous Indian destinations and discover their historical landmarks, attractions, and travel information.

The website currently features destinations such as Udaipur, Hyderabad, and Kolkata, highlighting their unique culture and tourist attractions.

✨ Features
Explore popular tourist destinations in India.
Dedicated destination sections for Udaipur, Hyderabad, and Kolkata.
Beautiful destination banners and images.
Information about historical places and famous landmarks.
Displays interesting facts about each destination.
Hotel price range information.
Simple and user-friendly interface.
Attractive design for an engaging browsing experience.
🏛️ Destinations Covered
1. Udaipur

Explore the City of Lakes, its beautiful palaces, lakes, and royal heritage.

2. Hyderabad

Discover the City of Pearls, famous for Charminar, Golconda Fort, and its rich Nizami history.

3. Kolkata

Experience the City of Joy, known for its colonial architecture, cultural heritage, and historical attractions.
