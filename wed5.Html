RAJASTHAN · THE CITY OF LAKES

Udaipur

Marble palaces on the water's edge, royal history at every turn, and the Aravalli hills standing guard over it all — Udaipur is India's most romantic city.

Heritage & Landmarks

Historical places to see

The palaces, temples and havelis that give Udaipur its royal Mewar character.

City Palace

An 11-palace complex built over 400 years by the Mewar dynasty, with mirror halls, crystal galleries and views over Lake Pichola.

₹300 entry9 AM – 5:30 PM

Lake Palace & Jag Mandir

A white-marble palace that appears to float on Lake Pichola — now a heritage hotel, reachable by boat from the City Palace jetty.

₹700 boat rideSunset best

Jagdish Temple

A 17th-century Indo-Aryan temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, just steps from the City Palace, known for its intricate stone carving.

Water & Nature

Lakes of Udaipur

Udaipur has no beach, but its seven interlinked lakes are its defining natural feature.

Lake Pichola

The city's central lake, built in 1362 — boat rides run from the City Palace jetty every day.

Fateh Sagar Lake

A quieter lake north of the old city with Nehru Park island and hillside viewpoints.

TELANGANA · THE CITY OF PEARLS

Hyderabad

Qutb Shahi forts, Nizami palaces, pearl bazaars and the country's most celebrated biryani — Hyderabad blends Deccan history with a fast-moving modern city.

1591Charminar built
10kmGolconda Fort walls
₹5–₹25kHotel range / night
Heritage & Landmarks

Historical places to see

From Qutb Shahi forts to Nizam-era palaces.

Charminar

Built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, this four-minaret monument is the symbol of Hyderabad, ringed by the Laad Bazaar market.

₹25 entry9:30 AM – 5:30 PM

Golconda Fort

A hilltop citadel once the capital of the diamond-trading Qutb Shahi kingdom, famous for its acoustic engineering and sound-and-light show.

₹100 entry7 AM – 6 PM

Chowmahalla Palace

The official seat of the Nizams, an 18th-century complex of four courtyards, chandeliers and a vintage royal car collection.

₹150 entry10 AM – 5 PM

Water & Nature

Lakes & river

Hyderabad has no sea coast — the nearest beaches (Visakhapatnam) are about 620 km away.

Hussain Sagar Lake

A vast man-made lake built in 1563, with the giant Buddha statue on Rock of Gandipet at its centre.

Musi River

WEST BENGAL · THE CITY OF JOY

Kolkata

The former capital of British India — colonial architecture, the Hooghly riverfront, trams, and a cultural life built on art, literature and food.

1921Victoria Memorial opened
705mHowrah Bridge span
₹1.6–₹14kHotel range / night
Heritage & Landmarks

Historical places to see

Grand colonial-era buildings and riverside icons.

Victoria Memorial

A white-marble monument built in 1921 in memory of Queen Victoria, now a museum set in 64 acres of gardens.

₹30 entry10 AM – 5 PM

Howrah Bridge

A 705-metre cantilever bridge over the Hooghly River, among the busiest bridges in the world — best seen at sunrise from a river ferry.

Free to crossSunrise best

Dakshineswar Kali Temple

A riverside Kali temple built in 1855 on the banks of the Hooghly, linked to the sage Ramakrishna.

Free entry6 AM – 12:30 PM, 3–8:30 PM

River & nearest beaches
